Acceptable Use Policy

Last Updated: October 3, 2026 · Effective Date: October 3, 2026

This Acceptable Use Policy specifies permitted and prohibited uses of the Kipakosa platform, printing services, and Augmented Reality cloud hosting.

1. Prohibited Media & Content

You agree not to upload, store, print, or distribute any media (photos, audio tracks, or videos) containing:

  • Illegal Content: Content that violates the laws of Nepal or applicable international laws.
  • Explicit or CSAM Material: Sexually explicit material, non-consensual imagery, or child sexual abuse material (zero-tolerance policy).
  • Hate Speech & Harassment: Content promoting discrimination, hatred, violence, or defamation against individuals or groups.
  • Copyright Infringement: Pirated media, trademark violations, or copyrighted materials that you do not own or possess express licensing to reproduce.

2. Infrastructure Abuse & Security

Users are prohibited from:

  • Attempting to bypass API rate limits, reverse-engineer proprietary tracking compilers, or flood our upload storage endpoints with automated bots.
  • Probing or scanning our servers (Oracle Cloud VM, Cloudflare R2, Vercel edge networks) for security vulnerabilities without prior written consent.

3. Enforcement & Account Termination

Kipakosa reserves the right to immediately cancel orders, refuse printing, and permanently delete hosted AR projects found in violation of this policy without refund.

4. Reporting Violations

To report a suspected violation of this policy, please contact our trust & safety team at kipakosa1528@gmail.com.
