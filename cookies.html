Cookie & Storage Policy

Last Updated: October 3, 2026 · Effective Date: October 3, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Kipakosa uses cookies, browser local storage, and similar web technologies when you visit our website or experience our WebAR scanners.

1. What Are Cookies and Local Storage?

Cookies and HTML5 Local Storage are small data files placed on your computer or mobile device by websites to remember user preferences, maintain session state, and understand traffic patterns.

2. Types of Technologies We Use

  • Strictly Necessary Storage: Used to maintain admin dashboard authentication tokens, secure session headers, and keep your creator studio upload state persistent.
  • Performance & Cache Storage: Used to cache WebAssembly AR neural networks and image feature descriptors in IndexedDB/browser cache so scanning loads instantly on repeat visits without re-downloading large target assets.
  • Analytics Cookies: Anonymized metrics via Google Analytics 4 to understand device types, page load latency, and geographic usage in Nepal and abroad.

3. Third-Party Tracking & Advertising

We do not sell personal data to third-party ad brokers or use invasive tracking pixels. Any marketing attribution (such as Meta or TikTok pixels) is configured in aggregated mode purely for campaign performance measurement.

4. Managing Your Preferences

You can control or clear cookies and local storage through your browser settings at any time. Note that clearing local storage may log you out of creator studio accounts or require reloading AR tracking models upon your next scan.
