Everything You Need To Know

AR Photo Frames & Living Prints in Nepal

Learn how augmented reality living photo frames work, explore frame sizes, and order custom gifts in Kathmandu and across Nepal.

What is an AR Photo Frame and how does it come to life? ▼

An AR (Augmented Reality) Photo Frame from Kipakosa combines high-definition physical photo printing with spatial web technology. When you point any mobile smartphone camera at the physical print, our browser-based neural tracker recognizes the image and instantly unlocks the matching motion video and audio seamlessly overlaid on the photo paper.

Do I or my gift recipient need to download an app? ▼

No app download is required! Kipakosa runs entirely on WebAR standards. Anyone with an iPhone or Android phone can scan the discreet QR code on the back of the frame, tap "Allow Camera", and enjoy the living playback directly in Safari, Chrome, or any standard browser.

How much does an AR Living Photo Frame cost in Nepal? ▼

Prices start from रू 190 for standard photo prints and रू 450 for complete tabletop frames (4×6 inch), up to रू 1,700 for large A3 wall gallery master frames. You can check our complete size and pricing breakdown on our Pricing & Size Menu.

How do I order a custom AR frame in Kathmandu or across Nepal? ▼

Simply message us on Messenger or Instagram @kipa.kosa_ar with your photo and video. We provide instant framing previews, accept payment via eSewa, Khalti, or bank transfer, and deliver directly to your doorstep across Kathmandu Valley and throughout Nepal.

What occasions are AR living frames best for? ▼

Our customers create living frames for wedding vows, anniversaries, birthday surprises, travel memories, baby first steps, memorial tributes, and customized multi-page AR magazine albums.
